# Instructions for agents

This repo is a GTM card (`index.html` + `card.json`) built from a small brain (`brain/`).

- `brain/` is the source of truth. `card.json` is generated from it.
- Never invent facts, numbers, tools or builds. If something is missing, leave a `TODO:` in the brain page and leave the card as it is.
- Only put things on the card that are fine to make public.
- Keep `card.json` within the shape in the README: up to 8 focus items, up to 8 stack groups, exactly 6 numbers, up to 4 builds. Short text, no em dashes.
- When updating the card, change only what the brain supports. Bump `updated` to today's date.
- Don't commit to `main` without the owner's OK. Open a branch or PR.
